# Football-Specific Youth Programming

## Session Architecture by Age (aligned to Football Australia / FA practice)

### 5–8 (MiniRoos / Foundation)
- 45–60 min, ball each player, game-themed activities, 4v4 matches.
- Physical content is hidden inside games: dribbling tag = agility; gate games =
  acceleration; 1v1s to small goals = change of direction.
- No fitness drills, no laps, no lines longer than 4 players.

### 9–12 (Skill Acquisition)
- 60–75 min: arrival ball mastery → technique block (passing, first touch, 1v1,
  striking) → positioning games → SSG.
- Add the ytkb-002 speed micro-dose (10 min, post warm-up) twice weekly.
- 7v7 → 9v9 game formats build positional understanding.

### 13–15 (Game Training, circa-PHV caution applies)
- Physical development becomes explicit: speed block 1×/week, movement-strength
  2×/week (ytkb-003 pre-loaded templates), SSG conditioning embedded.
- Monitor growth; reduce sprint/jump volume for players in rapid growth phases.

### 16–18 (Performance phase)
- Full integration: gym-based strength 2×/week, position-specific speed and
  conditioning, structured weekly periodisation around match day (MD-style):
  MD+2 recovery · MD-4 high load · MD-2 speed/sharpness · MD-1 activation.

## Position-Specific Physical Emphases (15+ only)

| Position | Physical bias |
|---|---|
| Fullback / winger | Repeated 30–40 m sprints, curved runs, crossing under fatigue |
| Centre back | Aerial timing (jump-land mechanics), short-burst acceleration, strength |
| Central midfield | Repeat high-intensity efforts, scanning + turn speed |
| Striker | 5–15 m acceleration, deceleration into finishing actions |
| Goalkeeper | Lateral power, dive-and-recover, short explosive sets, dedicated GK coach |

## Technical-Physical Blended Drills (highest training economy)

- **Rondo-to-break:** 5v2 rondo; on the 8th pass, ball played into space and two
  players sprint-duel 15 m to finish. Technique + acceleration in one drill.
- **Dribble gauntlet:** zig-zag cone weave (ytkb-002) performed with the ball,
  finishing with a shot — same agility cues, ball mastery added.
- **Transition waves:** 3v2 attack immediately becomes 2v3 defence on coach's call —
  repeated-sprint conditioning disguised as game realism.

## Sex-Specific Notes

- Mixed-sex football is appropriate and common pre-PHV.
- Post-PHV female players: embed FIFA 11+ (full version) every session — proven
  30–50% reduction in injuries; emphasise deceleration and cutting mechanics.

## Coaching-Culture Flags for the AI

- Check birth quartiles in any squad data: relative age effect inflates early-born
  selection. Recommend bio-banding or late-developer patience where detected.
- Players should touch the ball within the first 2 minutes of any session plan
  the AI generates for ages 5–12.
