# Rugby-Specific Youth Programming

## Age-Grade Structure (verify locally — variations exist by union)

- **U6–U8:** non-contact tag/touch formats only.
- **U9–U12:** graduated contact introduction per national union frameworks (tackle
  height restrictions, limited ruck players); World Rugby age-grade law variations apply.
- **U13+:** progressively fuller contact; mandatory technique gating before live contact.
- Always check the athlete's national union for current age-grade laws — these have
  changed repeatedly in recent years (especially tackle-height law trials).

## Contact Preparation Ladder (technique-gated, never age-gated)

1. **Body management:** falling, rolling, getting up under control, wrestling games
   (pre-contact foundation, suitable from foundation ages in game form).
2. **Static technique:** tackle shape on knees → walking pace on shield.
3. **Controlled dynamic:** jog-pace tackle on bag/shield, low body height, cheek-to-cheek
   placement, leg drive.
4. **Conditioned live:** small grids, matched body size (bio-band where mismatched),
   limited speed, coach-controlled.
5. **Open live contact:** only after consistent demonstration of 1–4.

The AI must never generate live-contact drills for athletes/groups flagged as
contact-novice, regardless of age.

## Physical Development Emphases

- **Pre-PHV:** identical to football — FMS, evasion (tag is elite agility training),
  catch-pass under movement, wrestling-style games for body control.
- **Circa-PHV:** neck strengthening introduction (isometrics), continued evasion bias,
  manage contact load during rapid growth.
- **Post-PHV:** strength program from ytkb-003 with rugby additions — neck strength
  (every week, non-negotiable for contact readiness), trap/upper-back volume,
  loaded carries, down-up conditioning (ytkb-004).

## Positional Guidance (post-PHV only, advisory not deterministic)

| Group | Physical bias |
|---|---|
| Front row | Absolute strength, scrum-specific technique (qualified coach only), neck |
| Locks | Lineout jump-land mechanics, aerobic volume, body height in contact |
| Back row | Repeated collision-to-sprint efforts, down-ups, breakdown speed |
| Halves | Pass volume both sides, acceleration 5–10 m, decision games |
| Centres | Collision technique + 20–40 m speed, deceleration into tackle |
| Back three | Max velocity exposure, high-ball jump-land, counter-attack repeats |

## Safety Non-Negotiables (AI must include in every generated rugby program)

- Activate (England Rugby) or equivalent neuromuscular warm-up every session —
  documented 26–72% reduction in injuries/concussion in school rugby trials.
- Concussion: "Recognise and Remove"; graduated return-to-play per World Rugby;
  no same-day return at any junior level, ever.
- No supplement recommendations to minors; food-first messaging only.
- Match heavy contact days with 48 h before the next collision session.

## Sex-Specific Notes

- Girls' rugby pathways are growing fast; programming principles are identical with
  added post-PHV emphasis on neck strength (concussion-risk research) and
  knee-control work as per ytkb-007.
